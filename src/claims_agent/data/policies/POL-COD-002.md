## Laterality modifiers
Procedures performed on paired body parts (knee, shoulder, hip injections, arthroscopy) need RT,
LT or 50 when the payer requires laterality. A missing laterality modifier is a correctable
coding denial; add the modifier and resubmit a corrected claim.

## Distinct procedural service
Modifier 59 or the X{EPSU} modifiers are used only when documentation supports a separate
session, site or encounter. Do not append them just to bypass an edit.
