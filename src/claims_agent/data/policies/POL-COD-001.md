## When to send a corrected claim
Coding and data-element denials (missing NPI, invalid subscriber ID, missing or wrong modifier,
diagnosis pointer errors) are fixed by submitting a corrected claim rather than a formal appeal.
Use claim frequency code 7 (replacement) and reference the original payer claim number.

## Checklist before resubmission
Confirm the rendering and billing NPI are present, the subscriber ID matches the eligibility
response, every service line has a valid diagnosis pointer, and required modifiers are reported.
Corrected claims must still be received within the payer's corrected-claim window.
