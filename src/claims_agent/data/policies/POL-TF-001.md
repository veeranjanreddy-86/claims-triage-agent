## Proof of timely filing
A timely filing denial can be overturned only with proof that the original claim was received
within the filing limit: a clearinghouse acceptance report, a payer acknowledgment (277CA) or a
payer portal confirmation showing the original submission date.

## No proof available
If the first submission was outside the filing limit and no proof exists, the balance is not
billable to the patient in most contracts and should go to write-off review.
