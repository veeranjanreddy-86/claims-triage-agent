## Diagnosis supports procedure
A diagnosis-inconsistent denial means the listed diagnosis does not justify the procedure.
Wellness or immunization codes as the only diagnosis on a problem-oriented visit or therapy
service are a frequent cause. Review the clinical note, select the documented condition,
fix the diagnosis pointer and resubmit a corrected claim. Appeal only if the original coding
was correct and the payer edit is wrong.
